About Jobconfirmation.Ai

Jobconfirmation.Ai was built on a simple idea: job seekers deserve to know that the roles they're applying to are real. Too many job boards are cluttered with expired listings, duplicate postings, and outright fake jobs that waste candidates' time and hope.

Our approach

Every role on our platform is reviewed before it goes live. Employers post directly, candidates apply directly, and applications reach the hiring team without middlemen or third-party redirects. What you see is what's actually hiring.

Who we serve

We work with employers who want a straightforward way to reach genuine candidates, and with professionals who want their job search to be built on trust rather than guesswork.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you apply to a role, we additionally collect your phone number, an optional cover note, and the resume file you upload.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To submit your application to the employer for the role you applied to
  • To notify you by email or browser notification when new verified roles are posted
  • To improve and maintain the platform

How we store your information

Your account data and applications are stored securely using Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Resumes are stored in encrypted cloud storage and are only accessible to you and our hiring team.

Third-party services

We use Resend to deliver email notifications, and Google AdSense to display advertising on this site. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at Google's Privacy & Terms site.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at jobconfirmationai@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Jobconfirmation.Ai is intended for professionals seeking employment and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
